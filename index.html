Responsible Disclosure

At the time of writing, all newly discovered vulnerabilities have been reported to their respective maintainers. We follow coordinated disclosure practices:

  • Vulnerability descriptions and affected source file names are publicly listed above.
  • Proof-of-concept exploits are withheld until after the camera-ready deadline.
  • Full disclosure will be coordinated with the respective maintainers.
